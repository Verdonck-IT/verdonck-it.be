Radio Cast – Privacy Policy

Verdonck IT · Last updated 21 October 2026

Radio Cast is an Android app that plays internet radio stations on your phone and on Sonos, HEOS and Chromecast speakers on your local network. This policy explains what the app does with data. In short: Radio Cast has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and sends nothing about you to Verdonck IT.

Data the app stores on your device

  • Your favourite and recently played stations, settings, chosen logos and the Pro unlock status. This stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • The last speakers the app played on, so it can offer to continue on them. This also stays on your device.

Connections the app makes

  • radio-browser.info, the community station directory, to search and list stations and their logos. When you play a station, the app reports a "click" for that station to the directory, as the directory asks of every client; this contains the station identifier and the app's name and version, nothing about you.
  • Radio stations: the audio stream is fetched directly from the broadcaster's server, which sees your IP address like any internet connection. Station logos may be loaded from the station's website.
  • Speakers on your local network (Sonos, HEOS, Chromecast): the app discovers and controls them over your Wi‑Fi. This traffic does not leave your network.
  • Google Play handles the optional "Radio Cast Pro" purchase. Verdonck IT receives no payment details; Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Data we collect

None. Verdonck IT runs no server for Radio Cast and receives no usage data, crash reports or personal information from the app.

Permissions

The app uses network access for streams and the directory, local network discovery (multicast) to find speakers, a foreground service and wake lock for background playback, and notifications for the playback controls. It does not use location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage beyond the pictures you choose yourself as a station logo.

Children

Radio Cast is intended for adults and is not directed at children.
